# Sensitivity Analysis — SIH 26120

This directory contains sensitivity analysis protocols assessing model robustness against reservoir parameter uncertainties:

1. **Permeability Uncertainty**: Varying formation permeability across $k \in [50, 500]\text{ mD}$.
2. **Oil Viscosity Sensitivity**: Testing temperature-viscosity Andrade coefficient variations.
3. **Steam Quality Variations**: Evaluating economic impact of surface boiler steam quality variations ($x \in [0.65, 0.95]$).
4. **Crude Price & Energy Cost Volatility**: Measuring optimization recommendation stability against crude oil price ($40 - $100/bbl) and natural gas fuel price fluctuations.
